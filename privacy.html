Getting started

How do I get my statements out of Lloyds?

Sign in to Lloyds Internet Banking on a computer, open your current account, and download your transactions as a CSV file. Lloyds only offers a limited stretch of history that way; for older months, download your PDF statements, which Sooner reads too. Then click Import in Sooner’s toolbar (or press ⇧⌘I) and choose the files. You can choose many at once.

Can I try it before I import anything?

Yes. On the first screen, choose “Explore with sample data”. It loads three years of made-up statements so you can see every screen. Delete All Data, in the File menu, clears them when you’re ready for your own.

What if I import the same file twice?

Nothing is counted twice. The import window shows how many payments are new and how many it already has before you commit.

Sorting your spending

A payment is in the wrong category. How do I fix it?

In Transactions, change its category. Sooner offers to remember the choice as a rule, so the next payment to the same place lands in the right category on its own. Your categories and rules live in Settings (⌘,).

What is Needs Attention?

The payees Sooner couldn’t place, usually people you pay. Only you know what they are, so it asks once.

How do limits work?

On Spending, choose Set a Limit, or right-click a category. A limit shows on that category’s row, against what you’ve spent so far, and says what keeping to it would buy, in time.

Your date

How is it worked out?

From what you actually take home, save and spend, measured from your statements, plus your age, your pension and roughly what you hold elsewhere. It finds the first month your money could carry you, with pensions opening when they’re allowed to and your State Pension at its age. Every figure is in today’s money. “What it rests on”, on Freedom, lists every input and where it came from.

Why did my date move?

When a pay period closes, Home shows what you spent against your usual and whether your date moved, and why. Changing your details, adding an account, or making a what-if real moves it too.

How do I make a what-if real, and undo it?

Trying a what-if changes nothing: your date stays put, and the what-if is laid over it. When you’ve actually decided, choose Make It Real. It lists what will change, does it, and leaves a receipt under “In your plan” with an Undo button.

Privacy policy

Sooner collects nothing. There’s no account, no bank login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app has no network access at all, so nothing in your statements can leave your Mac through it.

What the app keeps, and where

Sooner reads only the statement files you choose with the Open panel. Everything it keeps is stored on your Mac, in the app’s own sandboxed storage:

  • the transactions read from your statements: dates, descriptions, amounts and balances, and a record of which files you’ve imported;
  • your categories, rules and limits;
  • the accounts you add and their balances;
  • your planning answers: birth year, pay, pension contributions and what a year costs once you stop;
  • the what-ifs you try and the ones you make real;
  • small preferences, like the last screen you had open.

It doesn’t keep copies of your statement files.

What leaves your Mac

Nothing, through Sooner. The app’s sandbox has network connections switched off, it contains no code that talks to a server, and it uses no third-party services or software kits: no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or attribution. We have no servers that could receive your data, and we never see your numbers.

Where your Mac has Apple Intelligence, Sooner uses Apple’s on-device model to suggest categories for payees it doesn’t recognise. That runs on your Mac; nothing is sent to Apple or to us.

Backups

If you back up your Mac with Time Machine, your Sooner data is included like any app’s data. Sooner itself doesn’t sync to iCloud or anywhere else.

Deleting your data

In Sooner, choose File, Delete All Data… to remove everything it stored. Deleting the app from Applications leaves its storage in your Library; to remove that too, delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.nikolaoskontis.budgetmaker. Because we hold no copy, there’s nothing for us to delete on our side.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and use them only to reply. We don’t add you to a mailing list or share your message with anyone. Ask and we’ll delete the conversation.

Information from Apple

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Mac’s settings, Apple may give us aggregated figures such as how many people opened the app or how often it crashed. These don’t identify you, and they never include anything from your statements.

Children

Sooner is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.
